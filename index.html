What is kefir?

Kefir is a fermented milk drink made by culturing fresh milk with kefir “grains” — a living colony of friendly bacteria and yeasts. Over a slow ferment, those cultures transform the milk into a tangy, lightly effervescent drink packed with probiotics. It has been enjoyed for centuries for its gut-friendly benefits and creamy, refreshing taste.
